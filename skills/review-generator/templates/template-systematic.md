# Template: Systematic Review / Meta-Analysis (PRISMA 2020 Compliant)

Use ONLY when the user explicitly requests a systematic review, meta-analysis, or evidence synthesis. Load alongside `structure-common.md`.

## Overall structure

```
Title
Abstract (STRUCTURED: Background, Objectives, Methods, Results, Conclusions — 250–380 words)
Keywords (5–8)
[Registration: registry name + ID]

Introduction (flowing prose)
  → Background and rationale
  → Prior systematic reviews' limitations
  → Objectives (explicit PICOS/PEO question)

Methods
  2.1 Protocol and registration
  2.2 Eligibility criteria (inclusion + exclusion)
  2.3 Information sources (databases, date of last search)
  2.4 Search strategy (full search strings in appendix)
  2.5 Study selection process (screening levels, reviewers, disagreement resolution)
  2.6 Data extraction (items extracted, extraction form)
  2.7 Risk of bias assessment (tool: Cochrane RoB 2, QUADAS-2, ROBINS-I, etc.)
  2.8 Data synthesis (narrative, meta-analysis with effect measures, heterogeneity I²)
  2.9 Certainty assessment (GRADE)

Results
  3.1 Study selection (PRISMA flow diagram — mandatory)
  3.2 Study characteristics (descriptive table)
  3.3 Risk of bias results
  3.4 Results of individual studies (per outcome)
  3.5 Synthesis results (meta-analysis: forest plots, pooled effects, heterogeneity)
  3.6 Subgroup and sensitivity analyses
  3.7 Reporting biases (funnel plot, Egger's test)
  3.8 Certainty of evidence (GRADE summary of findings table)

Discussion
  → Summary of evidence, comparison with other reviews, strengths and limitations
  → Implications for practice, policy, and future research

Conclusions  ← allowed only for systematic reviews

References
Appendices (full search strategies, excluded studies, data extraction forms)
```

## Drafting order

Systematic reviews follow a rigid PRISMA structure — draft in logical consequence order:

1. **Methods** (protocol, search strategy, eligibility criteria — everything the reader needs to evaluate reproducibility)
2. **Results** (study selection flow, characteristics, individual study results, synthesis, PRISMA flow diagram)
3. **Discussion** (summary of evidence, limitations, implications)
4. **Conclusions**
5. **Introduction** (written last — background, rationale, PICOS question, informed by what the search found)
6. **Final assembly**: Title → Structured Abstract → Keywords → [Registration] → Introduction → Methods → Results → Discussion → Conclusions → References → Appendices
